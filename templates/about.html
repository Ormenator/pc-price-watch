How prices show up (legally)

Retailers control how their catalog data is accessed. We only collect live prices through official APIs you plug in:

  • eBay Browse API — free keys, UK by default, no eBay login needed.
  • Best Buy Products API — optional, US.

For other shops, search links open the retailer’s own search page in a new tab. Listings stay on the retailer’s site and are never scraped or copied here.

History lives on your computer. “Good price” is our memory of your hunts, not a mystery scraper.
